Call for Submissions · ଲେଖା ପଠାନ୍ତୁ

Submit Your Writings for Abhilasha Annual Magazine

We invite unpublished literary works in Odia (ଓଡ଼ିଆ) and Hindi (हिन्दी) from passionate poets, storytellers, researchers, and essayists to be published in our upcoming print annual edition.

Submission Guidelines

🖋️
Accepted Genres: Poetry (କବିତା / कविता), Short Stories (ଗଳ୍ପ / लघु कथा), Literary Essays (ପ୍ରବନ୍ଧ / निबंध), and Cultural Commentaries.
🌐
Languages: Odia (ଓଡ଼ିଆ) or Hindi (हिन्दी). Bilingual entries are warmly welcomed.
📄
Originality: All submissions must be the author's original, previously unpublished work.
🏆
Publication & Honor: Selected writers will receive a complimentary print copy of the Annual Magazine and feature in our literary directory.
