2. Personal Data We Collect (Purpose Limitation)

We collect only the minimum necessary personal data required to fulfill your orders and deliver a personalized reading experience:

  • Identity Data: Full name and username when registering or placing an order.
  • Contact Data: Shipping address, district, state, pin code, mobile number, and email address.
  • Transaction Data: Payment details (processed securely via Razorpay; we do not store full credit card or UPI PIN data).
  • Technical Data: IP address, device browser type, and essential session cookies for cart maintenance.
  • User Feedback: Reader stories, book ratings, or optional query messages submitted directly by you.

3. Lawful Basis & Explicit Consent

We process your personal data based on your explicit consent granted during account creation or checkout. The data is processed strictly for:

  1. Processing, dispatching, and delivering book orders across Odisha and India.
  2. Sending order tracking notifications via SMS, WhatsApp, or Email.
  3. Providing customer support and processing authorized refunds/returns.
  4. Complying with statutory accounting and tax obligations under Indian Law.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Processors

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We only share necessary data with trusted data processors under strict confidentiality contracts:

  • Logistics Partners: India Post / Verified Regional Couriers (strictly for delivery purposes).
  • Payment Gateways: Razorpay Software Private Limited (PCI-DSS compliant payment processing).
  • Cloud Infrastructure: Supabase PostgreSQL & Vercel (encrypted database & web hosting).

5. Your Rights Under DPDP Act 2023

As a Data Principal, you enjoy the following enforceable rights:

  • Right to Access: Request a summary of your personal data processed by us.
  • Right to Correction & Erasure: Request updating inaccurate details or deletion of your account/data.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw your consent at any time for non-essential processing.
  • Right of Grievance Redressal: Seek resolution for any privacy concerns via our Grievance Officer.

6. Data Security & Retention

Your data is stored securely using industry-standard SSL encryption (256-bit), HTTPS transport protection, and restricted administrative controls. We retain personal transaction records only for the period mandated by tax and corporate regulations.
