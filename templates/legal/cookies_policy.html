Cookies & Local Storage Policy

Effective Date: September 26, 2026

Bahi Hata uses cookies and HTML5 local storage technologies to ensure seamless site functionality, store your active reading shelf items, and deliver an optimized browsing experience.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your browser or device when you visit websites. They help remember your preferences, keep you logged in, and keep track of items added to your cart or reading shelf.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

1. Essential Session Cookies (Strictly Necessary)

Required for site login (`sessionid`), CSRF security protection (`csrftoken`), and user authentication. These cannot be disabled.

2. Functionality & Preferences Cookies

Remembers your language choice (English vs. Odia) and display settings.

3. Local Storage (Client-side Data)

Stores your client-side mini-cart items and cookie consent preference (`cookie_consent`) locally on your device for fast performance without tracking.

3. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You can modify your cookie settings at any time using your browser settings or by clearing your browser's site data. Note that disabling essential cookies may impact your ability to log in or complete orders.
