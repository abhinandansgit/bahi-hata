Refund, Return & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: September 26, 2026 | Bahi Hata Customer Protection

📦 100% Genuine Books & Reader Satisfaction Guarantee

Every book and magazine shipped by Bahi Hata undergoes quality inspection before dispatch. If you receive a damaged, misprinted, or incorrect title, we provide a hassle-free 7-Day Replacement or Refund Guarantee.

1. Return & Replacement Eligibility

You can request a return or replacement within 7 days of order delivery under the following conditions:

  • Book received with physical transit damage, torn pages, or misprinting.
  • Wrong title, edition, or combo item delivered compared to your order invoice.
  • Missing pages or missing items from a combo set.

Note: Books must be in unread, original condition with invoice details intact.

2. How to Initiate a Return

  1. Take clear photos or a short video showing the damaged or wrong item along with the parcel shipping label.
  2. Email our support team at bahihata2026@gmail.com or message us on WhatsApp at +91 63722 02830 with your Order ID and photos.
  3. Our team will inspect and approve the return within 24–48 hours and arrange for reverse pickup or send replacement instructions.

3. Refund Process & Timelines

  • Prepaid Orders (UPI / Cards / NetBanking / Razorpay): Refunds will be credited directly to your original payment method within 5 to 7 business days following return verification.
  • Cash on Delivery (COD) Orders: Refunds will be processed via direct Bank Transfer (NEFT/IMPS) or UPI upon receiving customer bank details.

4. Order Cancellation Guidelines

  • Before Dispatch: You can cancel your order at any time before dispatch via your Account Dashboard or by contacting customer support. Full refund will be issued immediately.
  • After Dispatch: Once the parcel is handed over to India Post or courier partners, cancellation is not possible. You may decline parcel acceptance upon delivery attempt.
